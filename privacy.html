Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

The Komen portfolio app and website show information about data science projects. They do not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the app stores

Only your light or dark theme choice, saved on your own device. It is never sent anywhere.

Contact form

The contact form opens your own email app with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent until you send that email yourself, and the app never sees or keeps what you type.

Links

Project links open GitHub in your browser. GitHub's own privacy policy applies there.

Analytics and ads

The app has no analytics, tracking, advertising or accounts.
